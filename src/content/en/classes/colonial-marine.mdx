<h2 id="overview">Overview</h2>

The Colonial Marine is the cleanest place to learn the rhythm of *Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2*. The class rewards steady aim, controlled positioning, and awareness of the other three Marines rather than a complicated setup. Your job is to keep a lane readable, remove runners before they reach the team, and save burst tools for the moment a stable defense starts to bend.

<Callout title="Field rule">If two Marines are shooting through your silhouette, the position is wrong even when your damage is high. Move half a lane and restore four clear weapons.</Callout>

<h2 id="role">Role in the Fireteam</h2>

Treat the Marine as the squad's dependable center. You are not required to chase every elite or hold every flank alone. Instead, connect the specialist roles: follow the Technician's control, focus the Recon's marked targets, and prevent pressure from reaching the Doc.

- **Primary job:** sustained damage into the main approach.
- **Secondary job:** fast response when a side lane breaks.
- **Avoid:** standing ahead of deployed defenses or blocking friendly fire.

<h2 id="early-priorities">Early Priorities</h2>

Spend early progression on consistency. Accuracy, magazine economy, and cooldown reliability create more value than a narrow damage bonus that only works during perfect waves.

1. Unlock one dependable rifle upgrade before spreading resources across several weapons.
2. Improve the ability you can use in every major engagement.
3. Keep one flexible perk slot for the mission modifier or squad composition.
4. Practice retreat routes before increasing difficulty.

<h2 id="loadout">Recommended Loadout</h2>

Pair a stable mid-range rifle with a close-range answer. The rifle owns the long lane; the secondary weapon exists to reclaim space when enemies cross the control line. Favor traits that reduce downtime and make recoil predictable.

| Slot | Priority | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Accurate rifle | Removes runners and exposes elite weak points |
| Secondary | Close-range stopping power | Restores space when the lane collapses |
| Augment | Magazine or reload economy | Keeps pressure continuous during long waves |
| Trait | Reliable conditional damage | Works without forcing unsafe positioning |

<h2 id="combat-loop">Combat Loop</h2>

Begin each encounter behind the first planned choke point. Clear small targets while the team establishes control, then commit burst damage when an elite crosses into overlapping fire. Reload during control effects, not after they expire. If the line breaks, fall back together; one orderly retreat is cheaper than four individual rescues.

<Callout title="Ability timing">Use abilities to protect a damage window or a retreat. Spending them on a nearly finished wave usually creates a cooldown problem in the next room.</Callout>

<h2 id="squad-tips">Four-Player Squad Tips</h2>

- Claim one side of the firing lane and keep it for the entire wave.
- Announce elites that leave the main approach instead of silently turning away.
- Let control classes trigger the engagement when their deployables are ready.
- Leave ammunition for the weapon that solves the squad's current weakness.
- When two teammates retreat, retreat with them; the old position is already lost.
